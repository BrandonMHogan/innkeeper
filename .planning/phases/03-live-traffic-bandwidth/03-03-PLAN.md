<objective>
Build the API-side half of the traffic/bandwidth pipeline: the trusted ingest route that receives capture's aggregated rollups (Plan 02's POST target) and writes both `traffic_flows` and `bandwidth_metrics` rows through the swappable `BandwidthSource` (Plan 01), the single global SSE broadcaster satisfying D-13's "one channel, full snapshot, fan-out" design, and the authenticated query routes for historical bandwidth (any time range, TRAF-02), per-device destinations (TRAF-03), and network-wide daily/weekly/monthly charts (TRAF-04) — all resolving a device's full MAC history (Plan 01's `device_mac_history`) rather than just `last_known_mac`, closing 03-RESEARCH.md's Pitfall 1.

Purpose: This is the only plan that turns stored rows into anything a user or frontend can observe — it is the seam between Plan 01's storage layer, Plan 02's data producer, and Plan 04's frontend consumer.
Output: `POST /api/capture/traffic`, `GET /api/traffic/stream` (SSE), `GET /api/traffic/bandwidth/{device_id}`, `GET /api/traffic/bandwidth/network`, `GET /api/traffic/devices/{device_id}/destinations`, plus the background snapshot-refresh task wired into app lifespan.
